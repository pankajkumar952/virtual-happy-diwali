About Virtual Happy Diwali

Virtual Happy Diwali is a free, interactive fireworks simulator that runs in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. Each rocket climbs with a sparking tail, then bursts into sparks that slow down, cool from white-hot to colour to a dim ember, and fall under gravity. It is a safe, smoke-free way to celebrate the festival of lights with family far away.

What you can do

  • Tap the sky to launch shells, and choose from 15 shell types.
  • Light virtual crackers: anar, chakri, ladi, atom bomb and phuljhadi.
  • Watch the Grand show, Today’s show (changes daily), the Countdown or the Finale.
  • Write a message in the sky and share it.
  • Pick a theme such as Diwali Gold, Tricolour or Rainbow.

Shell types

Peony, chrysanthemum, golden willow, kamuro, brocade, pistil, crossette, ring, palm, strobe, crackle, double blast, heart, star and spiral.

Frequently asked questions

Is Virtual Happy Diwali free?

Yes. No sign-up and nothing to download.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. For the biggest show, turn your phone sideways.

Why do I have to press Start?

Browsers block sound until you interact with the page, so Start lets the booms play.
